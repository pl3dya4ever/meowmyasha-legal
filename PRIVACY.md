# Meowmyasha - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meowmyasha ("the Bot") may collect, use, and process information when you interact with the Bot on Discord.

By using Meowmyasha, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features being used, Meowmyasha may process information provided by Discord or by users, including:

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs
- Discord Channel IDs
- Discord Role IDs
- Usernames and display names
- Server-related information
- Commands and interactions sent to the Bot
- Information submitted through forms, reports, or other Bot features
- Message content when required for a specific Bot feature
- Attachments or links voluntarily submitted through Bot features

Not all of this information is necessarily stored permanently.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Meowmyasha may be used to:

- Provide and operate Bot features
- Execute commands and user interactions
- Provide moderation and server management features
- Process reports submitted through the Bot
- Provide verification systems
- Provide welcome and community features
- Maintain configuration for Discord servers
- Detect abuse or misuse of the Bot
- Diagnose errors and technical problems
- Improve the Bot and its functionality

## 3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored when necessary for a particular feature or server configuration.

Information that does not need to be permanently stored may only be processed temporarily while performing the requested action.

The amount and duration of stored information may depend on the feature being used.

## 4. User-Submitted Information

Users may voluntarily submit information through features such as reports, forms, commands, or other interactions.

For example, a report may contain:

- The reporting user's information
- Information about another Discord user
- A reason for the report
- Evidence, links, or attachments provided by the user

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive or personal information through the Bot.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Meowmyasha operates through Discord and may use third-party services or infrastructure necessary to provide its functionality.

Information processed through Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Third-party services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## 6. Data Sharing

Meowmyasha does not sell user data.

Information may be visible to authorized server staff when required by a feature. For example, information submitted through a moderation report may be provided to the moderators responsible for reviewing that report.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or others from abuse or security threats.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed or stored by Meowmyasha.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Removal

Users or server owners may request the removal of stored information associated with them or their Discord server when applicable.

Some information may already have been automatically deleted or may not have been permanently stored.

To request data removal, use the contact information provided below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Meowmyasha is intended for use through Discord and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from users who are not permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements.
